Belcanto

A vocal coach with an honest ear.

Sing against any song. Belcanto separates the original vocal from the track, listens to your take, and diffs the two performances — pitch contour, phrasing, onset timing, vibrato — then says something true about the difference. Warm, specific, and honest. Not a pitch-bar game; an actual ear.

What does it mean to be a good singer?

Correctness is measurable. Expression is deliberate deviation from correctness — the scoop, the back-phrase, the note held a breath too long on purpose. Most tools score the first and punish the second. Belcanto is an attempt to model both, and to know the difference.

separate→track→align→diff→coach

Under the hood: source separation to isolate the reference vocal, neural pitch tracking on both performances, dynamic time warping to line them up, and a language model to turn the raw numbers into the kind of note a good coach would give.

Status — early workshop. The measurement engine is being built first; the opinions come later.
