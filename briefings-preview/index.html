Hank Speech Watch (dry run) — 21 Oct 2026

First pass on the new standing watches for Hank: Voxtral, Maltese speech, small on-device speech models, Mozilla Common Voice and Vuċi. The big story is Voxtral 3, which is API-only for now.

Mistral Voxtral

  • Voxtral 3 launches, API-only — Three speech-to-text models, a TTS model and a voice-agent SDK built on LiveKit. Mini costs $0.0035/min, Small $0.004/min and Realtime $0.008/min; TTS is $13 per 1M characters. Running it yourself means going through Mistral’s sales team, and the accuracy charts are Mistral’s own. No Voxtral 3 weights were on Hugging Face as of 04:10 CEST. (Mistral, Hugging Face, Hacker News 533 points, 20 Oct)
  • No Maltese figures yet — Mistral claims all 24 official EU languages, but its FLEURS charts leave Maltese out. (Mistral, 20 Oct)
  • Artificial Analysis ranks Voxtral 3 near the top — Small Transcribe 3 is #3 at 2.1% WER and Mini #5 at 2.2%, against MAI-Transcribe-2 at 2.0% and Scribe v2 at 2.2%. The test sets are mostly English. (Artificial Analysis, 20–21 Oct)

Maltese speech

  • Parakeet v3 scores 22% WER on Maltese, Whisper turbo 72% — Yap’s own small test on 100 FLEURS Maltese clips, where Maltese ranked last of 25 languages. NVIDIA’s published figure is 20.46%. Parakeet v3 is CC-BY-4.0. (Yap, 19 Oct)
  • Mozilla Common Voice: nothing new — The latest release is still Scripted Speech v27.0 (17 Sep). Maltese has 17.6 h (8.76 h validated), 225 speakers and 6,644 validated clips out of 13,352, all CC0. There’s been no tagged code release since v1.160.0 (21 Sep), no licence change, and no news on open-sourcing the spontaneous-speech platform. (Mozilla Data Collective, GitHub)
  • Vuċi: nothing new — The site still says its transcription platform is in closed beta. Its one public repo, licences, was last updated 2 Oct, and there was no press in the last 30 days. I saved a copy of the site and licence page to compare against from now on. (vuci.mt, GitHub, Google News, X)

Small on-device speech models

  • Gradium Phonon: tiny open-weight TTS from Paris — From a Kyutai spin-out, so it’s EU-origin. Phonon 31m is English, about 64 MB, Apache 2.0, with CUDA builds. Phonon 40m (5 languages, about 75 MB) is non-commercial only. The only quality test is Gradium’s own listening test. (Gradium, 20 Oct)
  • FUTO ASR4ALL: streaming speech-to-text at 28M–97M parameters — English only, with built-in voice-activity detection. It uses a custom licence with anti-surveillance clauses, so it isn’t OSI open source, and the benchmarks are FUTO’s own. (FUTO, 13 Oct)
  • Fermion Phonon-2: 164 MB English speech-to-text, CC-BY-4.0 — Built on Parakeet v3, with a 5.21% average WER across the 7 Open ASR test sets, measured by Fermion itself. There’s a CUDA container. (Fermion Research, late Sep)
  • Wake word: nothing new.
