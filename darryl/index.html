This is not "quit The Pub and gamble." It's a part-time path into security work: finding the weak points in systems before the wrong people do, and getting paid to say what you found. Start this week, with a device already in the post to you, and never miss a shift.

Why you

01

You read people

Every shift you clock who's trouble and who's about to walk the bill. Security is reading a system the same way.

02

Rugby taught phases

You think in attack and defence, in finding the gap before the line closes. Red team and blue team is that, with keyboards.

03

You can grind

A double shift on your feet is stamina most people lack. This work rewards patience over genius.

The one rule

Non-negotiable

Only test what's yours, or what someone invited you to test in writing.

Everything else is a crime, however good your intentions. Malta learned this hard: in 2022 some students found a flaw in a local app, reported it to help, and got arrested. The law has since moved to protect researchers who follow a company's published rules. The platforms below hand you that written permission from day one.

The first six months

A few hours a week, most of it free, all of it while you keep working. Nobody checks a certificate at the door here. They check whether you can do it.

The two lanes

Blue · defence

You hold the line

Watch the system, spot the intrusion, shut it down. Steady, in demand, the bulk of the jobs.

  • Monitor and respond to threats
  • Harden systems so they hold
  • Last-orders calm, applied to an incident
Red · offence

You find the gap

Probe a system you're hired to probe, find the way in before a criminal does, report it. Where the bounty money lives.

  • Test apps and networks on request
  • Think like the opposition, legally
  • Paid per flaw you find and prove

It's a contact sport. Offence probes, defence holds, and you study the other side's phases before they run them.

Where the work is, in Malta

iGaming

The island's biggest employer, every operator regulated to the teeth on security and fraud. Steady demand.

Fintech & blockchain

Malta leaned hard into financial tech. MFSA-regulated firms need security people, and pay for them.

Bug bounty, anywhere

No Maltese employer needed. Programs pay per finding, from your couch, in your own hours.

Legal tailwind

Malta is building legal cover for researchers who follow the rules. The field is opening here.
