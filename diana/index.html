A note from Matt

Hola, Princesa.

Princess Diana had to have her own page, didn’t she?

You said you wanted to get into AI. As it turns out, Malta built the best on-ramp in the world — and it’s completely free.

It’s called AI for Everyone (AI għal Kulħadd): a national course from the Maltese government, made with OpenAI and Microsoft. Malta is the first country in the world to offer it. It’s short — about two hours, self-paced, in English or Maltese, nothing technical. You log in with your eID and you’re off.

When you finish, you get:

  • An official certificate.
  • A free year of ChatGPT Plus or Microsoft 365 Copilot — the paid tiers, around €200 for the year. That’s the best part: you don’t just learn, you walk away with a year of the good version. (Nerdy tip: take the ChatGPT Plus option, not Copilot — you want the actual frontier model, not the Office assistant.)

Open to any Maltese citizen or resident with an eID, no age limit — there was even a piece about a 98-year-old who finished it.

Start at ai4all.gov.mt →

— Matt
