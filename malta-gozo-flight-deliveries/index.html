Fly it, kiosk to kiosk.

Drones hop between staffed kiosks on each island. The only sea leg is a short 6.6 km crossing that keeps clear of Comino. The recipient collects at the kiosk.

  1. 18.9 kmValletta to Ras il-QammieħPackage handed in, flown to the kiosk at the north-west tip of Malta.
  2. 6.6 kmSea hop to MġarrAbout 99% over open water. Battery swap at each kiosk.
  3. 4.5 kmMġarr to XagħraLast leg inland. Landed and handed over, never dropped.

Up to about 1 kg, 30 × 15 × 12 cm. Smaller than a loaf of bread: documents, keys, medicine, spare parts, a forgotten charger.

04 / 09

Why now

The aircraft work, the market has proved the model elsewhere, and the European rulebook says how to get approved.

780,000+Meituan drone orders by end of 2025

Across 70 routes in China and abroad. In Hong Kong, a Meituan route to Ting Kok village cut a 1.5-hour sea-and-hill delivery to 10 minutes. Zipline passed 2.5 million commercial deliveries by July 2026. [5][6]

2018Malta's Virtual Financial Assets Act

Malta has a record of moving early on new technology. Its regulator describes the 2018 crypto-asset framework as the first to regulate the sector this holistically. A small, well-defined drone route fits that habit. [7]

SpecificEASA category for this kind of flight

EU rules (Regulation 2019/947) give a defined approval path for flights beyond visual line of sight: a SORA risk assessment, then an operational authorisation from Transport Malta. [8][9]

€20k is tight, and we say so.

The authorisation fee and insurance are not yet known. If needed, we drop the spare batteries and recharge at each kiosk instead. That frees about €3.1k and adds about 45 minutes per stop during testing.

Test kiosks stay informal, for example in public parks, with the landowner's permission.

06 / 09

What could stop us, and what we do about it

Transport Malta Specific authorisation
MAIN RISK

Hire the SORA consultant first. Apply with the simplest case: one short over-sea hop, away from crowds.

Restricted airspace around Valletta

Launch from a permitted site. A courier carries the Valletta leg at first.

A ~10 kg drone over people

Rural kiosk sites. Routes over sea and open land, not over parks in use or town centres.

Staffing kiosks for battery swaps

One helper on each side during the pilot. Automated docks later.

Wind and weather on the channel

The north-west majjistral headwind is in the time budget. Fixed weather limits, with the ferry as fallback.

Rules on dropping cargo

The Open category bans dropping anything, so every package is landed and handed over at the kiosk.

€20k

Seed money for a pilot that earns the permit.

It buys one aircraft, the risk assessment and the licences. The goal is a Transport Malta authorisation for the Ras il-Qammieħ to Mġarr hop, and from there, paying routes.

  1. Now

    €20k seed

    Buy the Matrice 400 kit, get EASA certificates and operator registration, hire the SORA consultant.

  2. Next

    Pilot

    Test flights between informal kiosks with landowner permission. Log times, wind and battery use.

  3. Then

    Specific authorisation

    Submit the SORA to Transport Malta for beyond-line-of-sight flights over the channel.

  4. Later

    Paying routes, then direct

    First paying customers on the kiosk route. Later, a fixed-wing VTOL flying Valletta to Xagħra direct (est. ~€150k).
